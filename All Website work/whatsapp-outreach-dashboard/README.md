## 📋 How to Use the Outreach System

### Step 1: Customize Your Templates
At the top left of the dashboard, you will find two editable text templates:
* **Message 1 (Intro):** Introduces our creation (`https://drchavhanmultispecialitydentalclinic.com/`) and asks if they want to check it out.
* **Message 2 (Offer):** Emphasizes high-quality designs at cost-effective market rates.
* Modifying these boxes dynamically updates the WhatsApp buttons for all leads in real-time. Your edits are saved in browser memory (`localStorage`).

### Step 2: Import Leads
* **Method 1 (Copy-Paste):** Paste lead details directly from your Google Doc or sheet.
* **Method 2 (File Upload):** Download your Google Doc as a `.txt` or CSV file, and upload it via the drag-and-drop zone.
* Click **"Initialize Outreach list"** to load them into the Campaign Matrix.

### Step 3: Google Business Profile (GBP) Workflow
For businesses that do not have a number in your input:
1. Click the **"🔍 Open Google Profile"** link. It opens the Google Business Profile in a new tab.
2. Find the phone number listed on the Google listing.
3. Paste the phone number into the inline text box in that row and click **"Save"**.
4. The status changes to **"Ready"** and unlocks both WhatsApp buttons instantly!

### Step 4: Send & Track Outreach
1. Click **"💬 Send Intro (Msg 1)"**. It opens WhatsApp with the intro message pre-filled. Click send.
2. Go back to the dashboard, check the **"Msg 1 Done"** checkbox.
3. Once they reply or after a scheduled follow-up window, click **"🏷️ Send Offer (Msg 2)"** and check the **"Msg 2 Done"** box.
4. The Campaign Progress bar at the top updates automatically!
5. When complete, click **"Export CSV Data"** to download your updated lead spreadsheet back to your computer.
