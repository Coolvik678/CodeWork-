## Part A: Timestamped Scene Structure & Narration Outline

### Section 1: The Solar Hand & The Ancestral Secret (00:00 - 00:45)
- **Narration:** Look at your hand right now. Whatever shade it is, that color didn't come from nowhere. It came from one of the longest, most dramatic survival stories in Earth's history...
- **Key Concepts:** Sunlight absorption, African origin of all Homo sapiens 300,000 years ago, zero pale populations.

### Section 2: Skin as a Biological Shield (00:45 - 01:30)
- **Narration:** Skin isn't just packaging—it's your largest organ running dozens of jobs simultaneously. Its most critical job is protection from ultraviolet radiation...
- **Key Concepts:** UV radiation damage, Melanin as natural built-in sunscreen, Equatorial Africa selection pressure.

### Section 3: The Hidden Threat — Folate & Reproduction (01:30 - 02:30)
- **Narration:** UV radiation doesn't just attack skin cells; it destroys a vital molecule in your bloodstream called folate...
- **Key Concepts:** Double shield for dark skin: 1) DNA/Cancer protection, 2) Folate protection for reproductive survival.

### Section 4: The Great Migration & The Weaker Sun (02:30 - 03:30)
- **Narration:** Between 70,000 and 100,000 years ago, Homo sapiens began migrating out of Africa into Europe and Asia...
- **Key Concepts:** Low latitude UV drop, lower angle sunlight, shift from UV shielding to UV absorption.

### Section 5: The Vitamin D Crisis & Bone Health (03:30 - 04:45)
- **Narration:** Vitamin D is manufactured in skin when UVB rays hit cholesterol molecules. Your skin is literally a solar panel...
- **Key Concepts:** Vitamin D solar panel, rickets, childbirth mortality, depigmentation natural selection algorithm reversal.

### Section 6: Cheddar Man & The Neolithic Farming Shock (04:45 - 05:45)
- **Narration:** Ancient DNA shows early European hunter-gatherers, like 10,000-year-old Cheddar Man in Britain, still had dark skin and blue eyes...
- **Key Concepts:** Transition from Vitamin D-rich hunter-gatherer diets to agricultural cereal diets, causing a dietary Vitamin D collapse and rapid depigmentation.

### Section 7: Convergent Evolution & Blue Eyes (05:45 - 06:45)
- **Narration:** East Asians and Europeans evolved light skin independently using entirely different genetic pathways...
- **Key Concepts:** Convergent evolution, polygenic skin traits, single Black Sea ancestor for blue eyes.

### Section 8: Conclusion — Every Skin Tone is an Answer to a Star (06:45 - 07:30)
- **Narration:** Ultimately, skin color is a UV calibration system. Light skin isn't superior... Dark skin is the original ancestral baseline...
- **Key Concepts:** UV calibration system, baseline ancestral state, skin tones as biological answers to the sun.
